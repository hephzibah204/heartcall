# HeartCall With Mhideh 💖

> **Surprise Birthday & Love Calls in Nigeria and Worldwide.**
> Personalised live surprise calls, romantic messages, anniversary wishes, and heartfelt phone calls with Mhideh.

---

## 🌟 Overview
HeartCall turns deep feelings and thoughtful words into live, heartfelt phone calls. Each booking includes:
- 🎙️ **Live 2–3 Minute Call:** Delivered in English, Yorùbá, or a mix of both.
- 🎧 **Reaction Recording:** Sent to the booker to preserve the emotional reaction.
- 🕵️ **Anonymous Option:** Stay anonymous until the very end or throughout.
- 🌍 **Nigeria & Worldwide:** Accessible locally and across borders.
- 💬 **Instant WhatsApp Integration:** Seamless booking directly into WhatsApp.

## 🚀 Deployment

### Cloudflare Pages
Deployed via Cloudflare Pages:
- **Live URL:** [https://heartcall.pages.dev](https://heartcall.pages.dev)

To deploy updates manually:
```bash
npx wrangler pages deploy . --project-name=heartcall --branch=main
```
