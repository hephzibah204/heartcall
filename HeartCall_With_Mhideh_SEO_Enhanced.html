Before you book

Booking requests are sent to WhatsApp. We confirm availability and payment before the call. Times are in West Africa Time (WAT, UTC+1); international recipients may be in another time zone. Recording or sharing a call depends on the recipient’s permission and applicable law. If they decline, no recording should be kept or shared.

WhatsApp should open with your booking filled in. If it didn’t, tap here or and send it to 0810 791 8675.

Good to know

What happens after I send the form?

We receive it on WhatsApp, confirm the details and payment with you, then make the call at the time you chose.

Can I stay anonymous?

Yes. Tick the anonymous box and we will keep your name back until the end of the call, or as you prefer.

How long is the call?

About 2 to 3 minutes: enough time for a warm opening, your personal message, a prayer or wish, and a happy goodbye.

Which languages do you call in?

English and Yorùbá. Choose one, or both, in the booking form. Write your message in the language you want it delivered.

Do you call outside Nigeria?

Yes. Tick “outside Nigeria” in the form to see the international price.

What if they don’t pick up?

Tell us how you want it handled in your message, and we will agree the plan with you on WhatsApp.

Will I hear how they reacted?

Yes. Every booking comes with a recording of the call so you can replay the moment.
